Evo Life

Evo Life is Kelly Seale’s personal reminder tool on his own computer. Once a day, it checks the sender, subject and date of messages in his own Gmail inbox to notice new bill notices, then shows a reminder on his computer. It is not offered to the public, and only Kelly can sign in.

What it does

  • Checks one Gmail inbox, Kelly’s own, with read-only access.
  • Uses only a “new bill notice arrived” signal locally to show a reminder.
  • Keeps data on Kelly’s computer and the sign-in token in his password manager.

What it doesn’t do

  • Read message bodies or attachments.
  • Send, change, move or delete email.
  • Share message content or headers with anyone, including AI services.
  • Sell or share data, or use it for advertising.
