Privacy policy

Evo Life and Evo Google

Evo Life and Evo Google are Kelly Seale’s personal tools, operated on his own computer. They are not offered as a public sign-in service.

Google information and permissions

Evo accesses only the Google services Kelly authorizes. Enabled features may read calendar events, tasks, and contacts; create or update those records; send email; manage Gmail label definitions; and create or work with Google Drive files, documents, and spreadsheets created by Evo or explicitly authorized for the app. Optional YouTube access is read-only. Kelly grants these permissions in advance; each one is used only by a feature that has been built and turned on.

Evo Life’s bill-reminder reader remains separate. It examines Gmail sender, subject, and date metadata locally and produces a simple reminder signal. That reader does not fetch message bodies or attachments. To decide whether a reminder applies, only the resulting signal and its time, together with Kelly's own task title and reminder sentence (numbers masked), may be sent to an AI model; the sender, subject and all other email content are never sent.

Actions on Kelly’s behalf

Email is sent from Kelly’s own address and visibly identified as sent by Evo. Messages whose recipients are all on Kelly’s verified own-address list may be sent automatically. Other messages require Kelly’s approval for the exact message, unless he has approved a named process with specified recipients, a template, rate limits, and an expiration date.

Sharing and deletion use the same approval approach. Permissions granted by Google do not by themselves authorize an action. Kelly can disable an approved process at any time. Mail arriving at the Evo address does not authorize actions.

AI and approval services

When Kelly uses an AI-assisted feature, the minimum information needed for that feature may be sent to its selected provider: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or xAI. Sensitive numbers, recognized secrets, and unnecessary identifying details are removed or masked before transfer. Masking is not a guarantee that every sensitive fact can be recognized.

Only providers and account settings approved for this use receive Google-derived information. Evo does not use that information to train general-purpose AI models, for advertising, or for sale. If a required privacy check fails, the affected AI transfer is blocked.

If Kelly enables Telegram approvals, Telegram receives a brief action notification and approval link. Full message content and contact details are not included in those notifications by default.

Storage and retention

Google sign-in credentials are stored in Kelly’s 1Password vault. Working access tokens are held in memory. The connector does not place tokens in agent configuration files, prompts, or audit logs.

Read results are kept only in bounded temporary caches, subject to Google’s applicable caching rules. Pending actions are stored locally with access controls and encryption where required. Pending approvals normally expire after 24 hours. Full action content is removed within seven days after a terminal result, unless an unresolved outcome requires reconciliation.

The audit records action types, times, caller references, approval references, outcomes, and non-content identifiers. It excludes message bodies, subjects, attachments, contact details, document contents, and credentials. Detailed audit metadata is retained for 90 days. Minimal non-content replay-prevention receipts remain until Kelly resets the connector’s history.

Google retains sent messages and created records under Google’s own controls. Information deliberately included in an AI conversation may also be retained under that provider’s account settings; clearing the connector does not erase those conversations.

Control and deletion

Kelly can disable the connector, cancel pending actions, remove process permissions, and request deletion of locally retained connector data. Removing local records does not undo an email already sent or a share already granted.

Kelly can revoke Google access through his Google Account permissions. Revocation may disconnect both Evo Google and Evo Life because they use the same Google project.

Google Limited Use

Evo Life and Evo Google comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
