Privacy policy

Evo Life is Kelly Seale’s personal reminder tool on his own computer. It is not offered to the public, and nobody but Kelly can sign in.

Information accessed

Once a day, Evo Life checks one Gmail inbox: Kelly’s own. It uses read-only Gmail access (scope: gmail.readonly) and looks only at message metadata: sender, subject and date. It never reads message bodies or attachments.

How it’s used

The metadata is checked on Kelly’s computer to notice when a new bill notice has arrived. The result is reduced to a simple signal: “new bill notice arrived” and the time it arrived. To decide whether one of Kelly’s reminders applies, that signal, together with Kelly’s own task title and reminder sentence (with numbers masked), may be sent to an AI model (Anthropic’s Claude). The sender, subject, message body and all other email content are never sent. The reminder is then shown on Kelly’s computer.

What is never done

Evo Life never reads message bodies or attachments. It never sends, changes, moves or deletes email. Data is never used for advertising.

Storage and retention

Data stays on Kelly’s computer. The sign-in token is kept in Kelly’s password manager. Message bodies and attachments are never accessed or retained.

Sharing

Data is never sold or shared. No message content or headers (sender, subject, body) are shared with anyone, including AI services. The only thing derived from Gmail that leaves Kelly’s computer is the “new bill notice arrived” signal and its time, sent to an AI model as described above.

Your control / revoking access

Kelly can revoke Evo Life’s access to Gmail at any time through his Google Account permissions.
